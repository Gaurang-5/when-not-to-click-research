Qwen baseline configuration. The same-input local baseline pins Qwen2.5-VL-7B (Qwen Team 2025) through Ollama with model digest recorded, Q4_K_M quantization, deterministic decoding with seed 1729, context length 4,096 and a maximum of 180 output tokens; the prompt and JSON schema were frozen before the first response. It requests a forced box plus verbalised presence, uniqueness and box-confidence scores, which are prompted self-reports rather than native Jev probabilities. Inference runs serially on an 18 GiB Apple arm64 Mac. The local compute and energy price is unmeasured, so API charge and total economic cost are not equated.
