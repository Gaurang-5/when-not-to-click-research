# Agent safety research pack

**Gaurang Bhatia and Garvit Mundra**  
Prepared 28 September 2026

**Transferability of Safety Decisions in Autonomous AI Agents: Evaluating Probabilistic Decision Models Under Distribution Shift**

This is the central planning, evidence, and execution pack for the research project. Folders 01–09 contain the original professor-meeting and study-design materials. Folder 10 contains the active Jev safe-visual-grounding experiment, including real API outputs, the 2,000-task candidate dataset, human-annotation workflow, code, and results. The original source files remain preserved.

7. [Active Jev study](10_Jev_Safe_Visual_Grounding/00_START_HERE.md) — current status, runnable workspace, dataset, annotation, and next steps.

## First actions

- Complete two independent human annotations for the 951-item review queue and export raw Label Studio JSON.
- Measure agreement, adjudicate disagreements, and freeze the final labels before the external Jev evaluation.
- Give the professor the proposal, pilot report, source audit, and academic execution plan; ask for the college's required format and ethics guidance.
- Complete the missing institutional, authorship, resource, and budget details.
- Keep the paper's contribution scoped to calibrated click-or-abstain evaluation under target ambiguity; existing work already overlaps with calibration and threshold tuning.

The controlled pilot is complete, but the real-GUI dataset remains a candidate until human review and adjudication finish. The files do not establish ethics approval, a finished systematic review, or a completed publication study. Use the decision log to record agreed changes. Name order in this pack does not determine publication author order.
